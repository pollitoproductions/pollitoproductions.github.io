Vocabulario Español

Vocabulario Español is a free 3D game that runs in your browser. You are the keeper of a lost glossary whose pages have scattered across a quiet planet as glowing vocabulary cards. Explore the world, find all 1,000 cards, and learn each Spanish word as you collect it. Your progress is saved in your own browser, and there is no account or sign-up.
