Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers the Pollito Productions website at pollitoproductions.github.io, including its free Spanish learning games, practice tools, and the Pollito chatbot. It also covers our mobile apps, described briefly in section 6.

1. What this website collects

This website is a free learning site. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.

  • Game progress: Some games and tools save your progress (such as high scores or a saved game) in your own web browser, using local storage. That information stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data.
  • Chatbot messages: If you use the Pollito chatbot, the message you type is sent to Groq so the chatbot can reply. Your message is used only to generate that reply. Groq does not use your messages to train its models. Your teacher keeps a private log of chatbot conversations (the messages and replies, with the date and time) in a Google Sheet only the teacher can see, so he can check that students are safe and using the chatbot well. The chatbot never asks for your name, address, phone number, school, or any other personal information. If you choose to share personal information in a message, do not include anything sensitive.
  • Advertising: This website does not show ads — not on the public version and not on the classroom version. Advertising may appear in our Android app; see section 6.
  • Hosting: This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home or school addresses, or any other personal information from visitors to this website. We do not sell any information, and we do not share information with advertisers beyond what the third-party services in section 3 do on their own.

3. Third-party services used by this website

  • Groq (chatbot): Processes chatbot messages to generate replies. Groq does not use your messages to train its models. See Groq's privacy policy for how it handles that data.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting): Hosts this website. See GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles server logs.

4. Children's privacy

This website is used by students, including children under 13, as a free learning tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The chatbot is designed not to ask for any personal information, including a name. If you are a parent or teacher and believe a child has shared personal information through this site, please contact us at the email address below and we will help.

5. Data deletion

Because this website does not store your personal information on its servers, there is generally nothing for us to delete. Your game progress lives only in your own browser and can be cleared there. Chatbot messages are kept only in the teacher's private log described above — if you would like a message removed from that log, please email us at:

pollitoproductions.dev@gmail.com

6. Our mobile apps

Pollito Productions also publishes games and the Pollito app on Google Play, including Backrooms Breakout. Those apps may collect an anonymous user ID and high scores for leaderboards, and may use Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase for scoreboard storage. AdMob may collect device information, including an advertising ID, to show ads. For questions about data in our mobile apps, including deletion requests, please email the address above and specify the app or game title.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will show when it last changed.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at pollitoproductions.dev@gmail.com.
